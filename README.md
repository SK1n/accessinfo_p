## 🌟 Overview

**Access Info Public** 
The app lets people in Romania submit Law 544/2001 requests (freedom of information requests). To submit one, the user fills in a field, picks the institution, and chooses whether to get the reply by email or by post.

After that, they can see their requests, upload the institution's response, or file a complaint if there's no answer or only a partial one.
